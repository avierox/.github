# AVIEROX

### Aviation expertise, engineered for real operations

AVIEROX is an aviation company based in the Dominican Republic. We bring together operational flight experience, aeronautical consulting, avionics expertise, and software engineering to help airlines, aviation operators, and training organizations work with greater clarity and efficiency.

## What we do

- **Aviation consulting:** aircraft acquisition and fleet evaluation, operational planning, process improvement, and regulatory alignment.
- **Avionics and technical services:** navigation data, avionics configuration, system integration support, and operational readiness.
- **Aviation software:** AAMS for training and operational management, custom systems, integrations, and decision-support dashboards.

We begin with the realities of each operation, then design practical solutions that connect people, processes, and data.

## En español

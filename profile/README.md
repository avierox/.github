# AVIEROX

### Aeronautical consulting and aviation technology for real operations

AVIEROX E.I.R.L. is an aeronautical consulting and aviation technology firm specializing in strategic advisory, aircraft acquisition, avionics, and digital solutions for the aviation industry. We combine operational experience, aeronautical judgment, and software engineering to support airlines, aviation operators, and training organizations.

## What we do

- **Aviation consulting:** aircraft acquisition and fleet evaluation, operational planning, process improvement, and regulatory alignment.
- **Avionics and technical services:** navigation data, avionics configuration, system integration support, and operational readiness.
- **Aviation technology:** AAMS for training and operational management, custom systems, integrations, and decision-support dashboards.

We begin with the realities of each operation, then design practical solutions that connect people, processes, and data.
